### **Description** (Detailed)

Stop manually clicking hundreds of 15-minute slots on When2Meet! This extension syncs your Google Calendar with When2Meet to highlight and select your available times automatically.

**Features:**
*   📅 **Google Calendar Integration:** Sign in securely to fetch your busy times.
*   ✨ **Smart Autofill:** Automatically selects all free slots on the When2Meet grid.
*   🎯 **Custom Calendars:** Choose exactly which calendars to use (Work, Personal, etc.) or ignore.
*   ✅ **Reliable Saving:** Uses a robust 2-step process (UI simulation + direct server save) to ensure your availability is recorded correctly.
*   🔒 **Privacy First:** Your calendar data is processed locally and never sent to external servers.

**How to Use:**
1.  Open any When2Meet event page.
2.  Sign in to the grid (enter your name/password).
3.  Click the extension icon and sign in with Google.
4.  Select your calendars.
5.  Click "Autofill Availability" and watch it work!

**Perfect for:**
*   Students coordinating group projects.
*   Teams scheduling meetings across time zones.
*   Anyone tired of the tedious When2Meet grid!

**Privacy Policy:**
This extension requests read-only access to your Google Calendar events solely to identify busy time slots. Absolutely no calendar data is stored, shared, or transmitted to any third-party servers. All processing happens directly within your browser.
