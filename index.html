When2Meet Google Calendar Autofill

A Chrome extension to autofill When2Meet from your Google Calendar

What does this app do?

When2Meet Google Calendar Autofill is a browser extension that reads your Google Calendar events to determine when you are busy, then automatically fills in your available time slots on When2Meet.com scheduling grids. It saves you the tedious work of manually cross-referencing your calendar and clicking each slot. All calendar data is processed locally in your browser and is never sent to any external server.

Privacy Policy | View on GitHub

Features

  • Google Calendar Integration: Sign in securely with your Google account to fetch your busy times.
  • Smart Autofill: Automatically selects all free slots on the When2Meet grid, preserving any slots you already selected manually.
  • Custom Calendars: Choose exactly which calendars to include or exclude.
  • Privacy First: Your calendar data is processed entirely within your browser — nothing is stored or transmitted externally.

How It Works

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Navigate to a When2Meet event and enter your name.
  3. Click the extension icon and sign in with Google.
  4. Select which calendars to check, then click Autofill.
  5. The extension marks all your free time slots automatically.
