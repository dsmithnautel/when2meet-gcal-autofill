When2Meet Google Calendar Autofill

App Purpose:

When2Meet Google Calendar Autofill is a browser extension designed to save you time. Its sole purpose is to read your Google Calendar events (busy times) and automatically fill out your availability on When2Meet.com scheduling grids.

📄 View Privacy Policy | View on GitHub

Features

  • Google Calendar Integration: Sign in securely to fetch your busy times.
  • Smart Autofill: Automatically selects all free slots on the When2Meet grid.
  • Custom Calendars: Choose exactly which calendars to use or ignore.
  • Privacy First: Your calendar data is processed locally directly in your browser.
