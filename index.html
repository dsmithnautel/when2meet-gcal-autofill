When2Meet Google Calendar Autofill

When2Meet Google Calendar Autofill is a Chrome browser extension that automatically fills in your availability on When2Meet.com scheduling grids using your Google Calendar data.

Purpose

This application connects to your Google Calendar account using the Google Calendar API to read your calendar events and determine when you are busy. It then uses that information to automatically mark your available time slots on When2Meet.com, saving you the tedious work of manually cross-referencing your calendar and clicking each slot one by one.

How Google User Data Is Used

When2Meet Google Calendar Autofill requests read-only access to your Google Calendar data. This data is used solely to identify your busy time slots so the extension can determine when you are free. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your Google Calendar data is never stored, shared, or transmitted to any external server.

Features

  • Google Calendar Integration: Sign in securely with your Google account to fetch your busy times via the Google Calendar API.
  • Smart Autofill: Automatically selects all free slots on the When2Meet grid, preserving any slots you already selected manually.
  • Custom Calendars: Choose exactly which calendars to include or exclude.
  • Privacy First: Your Google Calendar data is processed entirely within your browser and is never sent to any external server.

How It Works

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Navigate to a When2Meet event and enter your name.
  3. Click the extension icon and sign in with your Google account.
  4. Select which calendars to check, then click Autofill.
  5. The extension marks all your free time slots automatically.
